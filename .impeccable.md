## Design Context

### Users
Christians, faith-based finance seekers, people interested in prophetic ministry (healing, deliverance, prophecy), and professionals seeking Myles's speaking or coaching services. They browse on mobile and desktop — often discovery-first via search (SEO-driven). Secondary audience: event organizers and media contacts vetting Myles for speaking invites.

### Brand Personality
**Three words**: Bold · Prophetic · Grounded  
Tagline: "Faith that grounds. Wisdom that compounds."  
Tone: Authoritative mentor who is equally at home in a boardroom and a pulpit. Confident without arrogance. Spiritual depth paired with financial precision. Not soft/devotional, not slick/hype-y.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Mixed — dark hero sections (#0f100a near-black), white content sections, orange (#ed5128) accent and CTA sections. Sharp-cornered, editorial feel.
- **Fonts**: Darker Grotesque (display/headings) + Inter (body) + Poppins (labels/secondary)
- **Accent color**: #ed5128 — a strong burnt orange. The one brand color.
- **Anti-references**: Avoid "cheesy Christian website" pastels and doves. Avoid "tech startup" blue-purple gradient look. Avoid generic motivational-speaker template sites.
- **Visual language**: Bold typography-first, minimal decoration, strong color blocking, sharp edges (rounded-none buttons), high-contrast.

### Design Principles
1. **Faith-first, not faith-adjacent** — ministry and belief come before the business credentials, not after.
2. **Typography does the heavy lifting** — Darker Grotesque at large sizes IS the visual identity.
3. **Color blocking over decoration** — section backgrounds create rhythm, not cards or ornaments.
4. **Sharp edges everywhere** — rounded-none buttons, hard photo crops, no soft shadows.
5. **Every page earns its SEO** — content is dense and keyword-intentional, not thin.
